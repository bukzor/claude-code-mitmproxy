# promotion.filed

A prompt copy no committed fixture covered was filed as one, and the line
names its nearest sibling -- the committed fixture with the smallest diff --
with that diff beneath it, or past `GLANCE_LINES` the command that shows
it. `committed N fixtures` follows once the hook accepts, and asks nothing.

The glance is for flawed deduplication: a fixture a few lines from its
sibling, differing in text a session switched on or a mask should have
neutralized, is two copies of one prompt. Addressing that is a `blocks.d/`
or `masks.d/` rule for the text, after which the two cores collapse and the
later fixture is redundant (`design/040-design.kb/fixture-lifecycle.md`). A
diff that reads as upstream editing prose is the ordinary case and asks
nothing: the fixture is what `check_patches` now verifies every patch against.
