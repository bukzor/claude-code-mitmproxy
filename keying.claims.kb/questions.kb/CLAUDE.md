# questions.kb -- maintenance guide

The three questions this ledger was built to settle, each stated twice:
as it was asked, and as it turns out to be answerable. The gap between
those two statements is the finding, not a preamble to it -- in all three
cases the well-posed form is a different question from the experienced one,
and in two of the three it has a decisive answer where the experienced form
only invited a preference.

- `prior:` `../this-proxy.kb`
- `ontology:` question, experienced form, well-posed form, settled by,
  residue, finding
- `defeated by:` a question whose two forms coincide -- then this
  collection is a table of contents and should be deleted in favour of the
  entry point

## What belongs here

A question, its two statements, which claims settle it, and what stays
open. Nothing else: the arguments live in the theories, and a question file
that argues is a claim in the wrong collection.

## What does NOT belong here

- New content. Every assertion here must be a citation. If settling a
  question needs a claim that does not exist yet, write the claim in its
  theory first.
