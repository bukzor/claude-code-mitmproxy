# Root-cause candidates

One competing explanation per file; frontmatter per
`../root-cause.jsonschema.yaml`. `../root-cause.md` is the decision point.

Belongs: a mechanism that could account for the observed incident, with
its supporting and contradicting findings.
Does not belong: the findings themselves (`../findings.kb/`).

Adjust `status` as evidence lands. At most one `leading` at a time. When
the decision closes, rewrite `../root-cause.md` and keep these files as
the record of why alternatives lost.
